Pandora’s box is a prominent Greek mythological artifact from Hesiod’s Works and Days, 
symbolizing a source of unforeseen, endless trouble initiated by curiosity. Zeus gifted the first woman,
Pandora, a jar (later mistranslated as a box) filled with misery, which she opened, releasing all evils—sickness, 
death, and greed—upon humanity, leaving only hope trapped inside. Something's in the way ...  Something's in the way ...  Something's in the way ...
